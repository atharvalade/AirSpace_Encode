# AirSpace: Tokenizing Air Rights on the Blockchain

## 🚀 Overview
Did you know that you **own the air rights above your property**? With **AirSpace**, you can **buy, sell, and lease** these rights, creating a new market for premium locations like **Miami Beach, Times Square, and beyond**. We bring **real estate into Web3** with an end-to-end decentralized solution powered by **Rootstock (RSK)** and **HyperScan**.

## 🏗 How It Works
1. **Mint NFTs** – Property owners tokenize air rights as **NFTs on Rootstock**.
2. **Secure Transactions** – Smart contracts handle leasing, buying, and selling with **Rootstock's secure, Bitcoin-backed network**.
3. **HyperSync & HyperIndex** – Real-time data indexing, blazing-fast marketplace listings, and ownership tracking.
4. **Legal & Financial Automation** – We generate legal documents, facilitate payments via escrow, and transfer NFTs seamlessly.

## 🔥 Why AirSpace?
- **First-of-its-kind**: No existing marketplace tokenizes air rights at this scale.
- **$30B+ untapped market**: Monetize airspace in high-demand urban areas.
- **Secure & cost-effective**: Rootstock's **low fees & Bitcoin security**.
- **Seamless experience**: From minting to legal finalization, everything is **automated**.
- **Blazing fast retrieval**: **HyperScan** powers real-time data for listings & transactions.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Blockchain**: Rootstock (RSK) – EVM-compatible, Bitcoin-secured.
- **Data Indexing**: HyperSync & HyperIndex – Instant market insights.
- **Smart Contracts**: Solidity, deployed on Rootstock.
- **Frontend**: Next.js & TailwindCSS for a seamless UI.

## 🔑 Key Features
- **NFT-based Air Rights** – Own, trade, and lease airspace.
- **Decentralized Marketplace** – Trustless, escrow-backed transactions.
- **Automated Legal Docs** – Smart contracts generate & enforce agreements.
- **Instant Property Listings** – HyperScan ensures lightning-fast updates.
